About the site

About MP3 MIDI Tool

A focused converter and a set of practical guides for moving between audio and MIDI.

What this site does

MP3 MIDI Tool provides a browser-based MP3-to-MIDI converter and detailed articles for two related jobs: extracting an editable note draft from recorded audio and rendering a MIDI performance as an MP3 file.

The converter runs the audio analysis inside the visitor's browser. An MP3 selected in the tool is not uploaded to the site. The resulting MIDI file is generated on the same device and is available for the visitor to download.

Why the guides are detailed

Audio-to-MIDI and MIDI-to-audio work rarely ends with one button. The guides preserve concrete settings, software versions, error messages, and recovery steps so a reader can diagnose a silent export, misplaced notes, tempo errors, or a conversion that does not finish cleanly.

Who operates it

MP3 MIDI Tool is created and operated by CHENGMAN. The site is maintained as an independent practical resource for musicians, arrangers, and anyone who needs to move material between recorded audio and editable MIDI.
