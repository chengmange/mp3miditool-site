Privacy Policy

This policy explains what happens to audio files, technical request data, and advertising data when you use MP3 MIDI Tool.

Audio files stay on your device

The MP3-to-MIDI converter processes the file you select inside your browser. The selected audio file is not uploaded to MP3 MIDI Tool, and the site does not store a copy of it. The generated MIDI file is created locally and is downloaded directly by you.

Technical data

The site's hosting and security provider may process routine technical request data needed to deliver and protect the site. This can include an IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, request times, and diagnostic or security logs.

MP3 MIDI Tool does not provide user accounts and does not ask for payment card details. If you contact the site by email, the message and address you provide are used to read and respond to that communication.

Analytics

The site may use aggregated web analytics supplied through its hosting provider to understand page traffic and technical performance. Those measurements concern site visits and delivery performance; they do not receive the MP3 file selected in the converter.

Third-party advertising

MP3 MIDI Tool may display third-party advertising. Advertising providers may use cookies, local storage, device identifiers, or similar technologies to deliver, limit, measure, or personalize advertisements, subject to their own privacy policies and any consent choices presented to you.

No third-party advertising is displayed on the site as of the last-updated date above. This policy already describes the data practices that may apply after an approved advertising provider is added.

External services and links

Pages may link to other websites or rely on hosting infrastructure operated by third parties. Their handling of data is governed by their own policies. MP3 MIDI Tool is not responsible for the privacy practices of an external site.

Your choices

You can leave the site without selecting an audio file, avoid contacting the site by email, and use browser controls to clear or restrict cookies and site storage. Blocking some storage technologies may affect future advertising or measurement features.

Policy updates

This page may be revised when site features, hosting, analytics, or advertising arrangements change. The latest revision date is shown at the top of the page.
